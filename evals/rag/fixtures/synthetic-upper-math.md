# Fraction Sense

<!-- page: 1 -->

## Common denominators

When two fractions name parts of different sizes, first rewrite them so the parts have a common denominator. After the denominator is common, combine only the numerators and keep the shared denominator. A coaching prompt can ask the learner what equal-sized parts both fractions can use.

## Compare before calculating

Before calculating, estimate whether each fraction is less than, equal to, or greater than one half. The estimate gives a reasonableness check after the calculation. A useful teaching prompt asks which benchmark makes the comparison easiest.

## Area and perimeter

Perimeter measures the distance around a shape. Area measures how much surface is inside the boundary. Two rectangles can share a perimeter while having different areas, so a learner should identify which quantity the question asks for before choosing an operation.
